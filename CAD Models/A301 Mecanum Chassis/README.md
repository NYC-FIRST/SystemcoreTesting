# A301 Mecanum Chassis

> [!NOTE]
> This folder documents a sample mecanum chassis built during the first week of the internship to test the new Systemcore and Motioncore combination.

## Project Overview

This mecanum chassis was designed and assembled in one week during the first week of the internship. It was made as a simple test platform for the new **Systemcore and Motioncore** setup, along with the **18-volt battery pack**.

The chassis uses four mecanum wheels so it can drive forward, backward, sideways, and diagonally. The goal of this project was to build a working mecanum base quickly and provide a practical platform for testing the controls, motors, battery, and overall layout.

## Final Metal Chassis

During the final week of the internship, the chassis parts were cut out of metal and assembled into the final physical version. The stronger metal base allowed the **nose drawer** to be attached on top of the chassis.

This folder focuses on the mecanum chassis itself. It includes the original CAD work and will also include images of the CAD model and the completed real-life chassis.
